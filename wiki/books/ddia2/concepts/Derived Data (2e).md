# Derived Data

Data that can be recreated from another source: indexes, caches, materialized views, search indexes, warehouses. Distinguishing it from the system of record is the single most clarifying move in the book's data-integration chapter.

See [[Combining Specialized Tools by Deriving Data (2e)]] and [[Unbundling Databases (2e)]].
