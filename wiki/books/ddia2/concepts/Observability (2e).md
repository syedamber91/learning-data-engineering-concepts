# Observability

Being able to tell what a running system is doing from the outside — metrics, logs, traces, and the ability to ask questions you didn't anticipate when you deployed.

The book treats it as a maintainability property: see [[Operability - Making Life Easy for Operations (2e)]]. It matters most where the failure is emergent rather than local — [[Feedback Loops (2e)]] is the ethical version of the same problem.
