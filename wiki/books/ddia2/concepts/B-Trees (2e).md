# B-Trees

The mutable, page-oriented index structure behind most relational databases: fixed-size pages, in-place updates, and a write-ahead log to survive a crash mid-split.

See [[B-Trees (2e)]] and the head-to-head in [[Comparing B-Trees and LSM-Trees (2e)]].
