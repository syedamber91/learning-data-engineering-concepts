# Byzantine Faults

Nodes that lie, rather than merely crash or go slow. Most datacenter systems assume these away; blockchains and certificate transparency are the notable exceptions the book points at.

See [[Byzantine Faults (2e)]] and [[Trust, but Verify (2e)]].
