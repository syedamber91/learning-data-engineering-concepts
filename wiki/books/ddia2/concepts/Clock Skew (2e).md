# Clock Skew

The unavoidable disagreement between machines' time-of-day clocks, and the reason timestamps make a treacherous basis for ordering events or expiring leases.

See [[Unreliable Clocks (2e)]] and [[Relying on Synchronized Clocks (2e)]].
